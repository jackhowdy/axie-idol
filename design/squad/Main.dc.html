THE ONE NEW IDEA — the squad has a name
Today a guest is "Guest-7F2A". With this name, the first thing a player does is name their squad ("Hill Runners"), and that name is what appears on every post, on the ladder and on the share card. It costs nothing server-side (posts already carry a free-text author label) and it turns an anonymous guest into someone a judge can root for. Kids name things. Let them.
