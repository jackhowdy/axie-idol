Ten traits, three per Axie
Rolled at hatch. 120 combinations. Traits decide what it asks for, what it notices, how it talks, and what it needs. Class sets the tone on top: Beast loud, Aquatic curious, Plant gentle, Bird chirpy, Bug chatty, Reptile dry.

HOW IT ROLLS
Three traits, no opposites together (Explorer/Homebody, Show-off/Shy). The first trait is the loudest and leads the voice.
Egg time nudges it: eggs snapped in many places lean Explorer, eggs that travelled far between snaps lean Athlete. So the way you carried the egg shapes who comes out.
Owned Axies roll from their class and parts, so a Mystic Aquatic reads differently from a plain one. Traits show on the buddy chip and the Idol ladder.
Revealed as speech at hatch: the three traits appear one at a time as the Axie says its first three lines. Fixed for life after that, except one EARNED trait at bond 10, chosen from how you actually played (lots of night snaps earns Night owl, lots of group shots earns Socialite).
